3. Import your contacts

Upload CSV, TSV, or a JSON array of contacts. Excel and Google Sheets can export CSV. Files are read in your browser; loaded contacts are saved in this browser. Queued drafts are saved by your local server. Map your columns, preview the contacts, then load them into the same templates and queue. Importing never sends mail automatically.

4. Find contacts and verify mailboxes

Find up to three relevant named contacts per company, then verify their mailboxes before sending. SMTP verification checks mailbox responses and catch-all domains; unknown, risky and invalid results cannot auto-send. Your old “checked” labels are only format/domain checks. Verification reduces bounces but cannot guarantee delivery. Paste your own Apify API token. Lookups run on your Apify account. Locally, the token is also used temporarily by the server to import the completed verification evidence; the token is never saved.

New lookups skip already sent companies and recipients. Mailbox verification also checks waiting drafts, excluding completed recipients. Leads Finder searches up to 33 company domains with a maximum of 100 leads per run, capped by the budget above. We keep up to three contacts per company. Newly found contacts are drafts until you review and queue them.

Check domains screens address format and mail-domain records with the original validator. “Checked (domain)” does not confirm that the person's mailbox exists; Verify mailboxes is still required for automatic sending.

Mailbox verification uses BounceVerify. Test up to 10 first to compare new candidates with your configured sender mailboxes and a few known hard bounces. The test sends no messages and cannot release queue drafts.

5. Review and auto-send

Unverified addresses can bounce. Known invalid, risky, disposable, bounced and blocked addresses are excluded. Catch-all results do not confirm the individual mailbox and can bounce. Unknown results remain held unless unverified sending is enabled.

Each selected account sends one different email in a shared batch every two minutes: up to three messages, then a two-minute pause. If a mailbox sent recently, the next batch waits until it is ready. Review opens compact routes so you can see the recipient and planned sender without scrolling through every draft.

Messages are marked sent automatically only after the sending mailbox accepts them. The same recipient cannot be queued twice. Inbox placement cannot be guaranteed; personal, relevant messages and accurate addresses help, while guessed addresses may bounce.
